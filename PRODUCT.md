# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Indonesian shoppers (buyers) as the primary target, followed by sellers. The users may have low digital literacy, meaning they rely on strong visual cues, icons, and step-by-step guidance rather than reading long texts.

## Product Purpose
An accessible, fast, and trusted e-commerce marketplace ("Uchinagastore") that makes buying and selling frictionless, even for users who need extra handholding.

## Brand Personality
Affordable (murah), Fast (cepat), Trusted (terpercaya). Approachable, guiding, and highly recognizable without feeling intimidating.

## Anti-references
- Do NOT use Shopee's exact color palette (orange), even though the layout recognizability is desired.
- Do NOT use text-heavy, abstract, or complex corporate layouts.
- Do NOT rely on subtle UI cues; everything needs to be obvious.

## Design Principles
- **Guide, Don't Explain:** Use clear iconography, stepped processes, and highly visible primary actions instead of paragraphs of instructions.
- **Speed is Trust:** The interface must feel lightweight and instantly responsive to convey reliability.
- **Familiarity with a Twist:** Borrow the universally understood, high-conversion layouts of top marketplaces (like Shopee), but carve out a distinct, non-derivative visual identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Low Literacy Optimized:** Rely on universal icons, clear color contrast for actions, and extremely direct error/success states.
- High color contrast for essential actions and navigation.
